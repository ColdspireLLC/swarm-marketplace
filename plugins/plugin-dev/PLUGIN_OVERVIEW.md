Teaches your agents to build Swarm plugins, and to list them in Swarm's marketplace.

It adds two skills. **swarm-plugins**: making a plugin with `swarm plugin new`, what a plugin can add for agents and to the window, running it linked as you change it, and testing it headless, with screenshots of Swarm's real window. **submit-a-plugin**: getting one ready for the marketplace, its listing and its release, then the catalog's entry and the pull request, asking you before anything is pushed or published.

It asks only to give the agents its skills, and runs nothing.
