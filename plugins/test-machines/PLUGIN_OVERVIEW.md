Shared test machines for your agents: the computers joined to Swarm, Android phones and iPhones, and bb's machines, which several threads use one thread at a time on a machine's screen. Agents get tools to look at a screen, read it as named elements, click and type, run commands, install and launch apps, read an app's logs, copy files, and record a flow as a video for their reply. Each machine has a line: a call that needs the screen waits its turn, and a thread's turn ends when its turn of the conversation does. Plain commands, file copies and screenshots run beside whoever has the machine.

You come first. Use it myself in the Machines panel, or swarm test-machines take, gives you a machine: the agent on it is cut off and told why, and the rest wait until you give it back. Agents can't take a machine for you. A computer or phone joined to Swarm has Open too: its screen live in Swarm's window, to use as if you sat at it.

Agents stay out of sensitive apps: passwords, banking and payments, mail, messages, photos, health, the car, social posting and shopping, and any you add. Opening one is refused, a step that lands in one is undone, and no screenshot is taken while one is in front. On the phones you carry, agents may only install and uninstall your test builds and read their logs.

Machines come from the drivers you turn on: the computers joined to Swarm (their screens, input, apps, files and recordings, with nothing to install), Android phones through adb, iPhones through WebDriverAgent, which the plugin builds and starts on the Mac they're plugged into, computers over ssh, bb's test machines, or a demo machine to try it with. Phones plugged into this computer or one joined to Swarm show up on their own, each turned on with one button, and an iPhone's WebDriverAgent is built with another, signed for the team of the Mac's certificate. It runs the programs those need (adb, ssh, xcrun and the like) on this computer and the machines joined to Swarm.
