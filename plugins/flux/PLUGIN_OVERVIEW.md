Your Flux issues, in Swarm, for you and for your agents.

**For the agents**: `swarm flux` reads and updates issues, as bb's agents do with `bb flux`: list them, read one with its comments and images, create, comment, move it along. A skill tells them how, and to track planned work in Flux rather than in Markdown files.

**For a thread**: attach it to an issue (`swarm flux start ABC-12`, or its Flux panel) and its agent keeps the issue current. A thread started from an issue is linked both ways, and the issue's status follows the thread's merge request.

**For you**: Flux's board and list, with its keys, its property palette and drag and drop; each issue's page with its description and comments in Swarm's editor, sub-issues filed in place, and Start agent, which hands the issue and its pictures to a new thread; issues to pick up on the new-thread page; changes made offline, sent when Flux is back; several Flux connections on its own page of Settings; and `::flux{key="ABC-12"}` showing an issue's card in a reply.

It asks to reach your Flux (the address you set), to keep its copy of the workspace in its own storage, to start threads, to read Swarm's threads, so a thread's issue follows it, and to suggest issues when you mention one in the composer. Connect it on its page of Settings (Settings, Plugins, Flux) with your Flux's address and an API token (Flux: Settings, API tokens).
